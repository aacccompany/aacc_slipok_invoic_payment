4 Easy Steps to Automate Payments

Step 1

Configuration & Setup

Navigate to Settings > SlipOK Invoice Payment. Enter your LINE Messaging API credentials (Channel Secret & Access Token) and your SlipOK Branch details. You can also upload your company's Payment QR Code here.

(Image Placeholder: Backend Settings Page)

Step 2

Send Bill via LINE

Create a standard Customer Invoice. Ensure the customer has linked their LINE account. Once posted, simply click the new "Send Bill via LINE" button. The invoice details and payment QR code will be pushed directly to the customer's chat.

(Image Placeholder: Invoice form with Send button)

Step 3

Customer Uploads Bank Slip

The customer reviews your beautiful LINE Flex Message and scans the QR code to pay. After payment, they simply send the Bank Transfer Slip image back right into the LINE Chat.

(Image Placeholder: LINE Chat showing the bill and QR)

Step 4

AI Verification & Auto-Reconcile

The Odoo Webhook catches the image and sends it to the SlipOK API. The AI verifies the exact amount, checks for duplicate slips, and ensures the money went to your bank account. Upon success, Odoo registers the payment, marks the invoice as PAID, and the LINE Bot replies with a receipt confirmation.
